Fitness covers all gym-based training practices: strength training, cardio, group classes and personal coaching. It is one of the most common categories in venue directories, with establishments in nearly every city.

::silo-links{style="fitness"}

## What is fitness

Fitness refers to all physical activity practiced for health, shape and well-being: strength, endurance, mobility. A typical fitness gym offers a weights area, cardio machines and coached group classes, from gentle wake-up sessions to high-intensity formats.

## How to choose your gym

Three things matter for a beginner: proximity (a gym under fifteen minutes away dramatically improves attendance), coaching quality (a trainer present to correct your form) and membership flexibility (no long commitment at first).

::tip
Visit the gym at the hours you plan to come: the crowd changes everything between 7 a.m. and 7 p.m.
::
